# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Premier League football fans, tactical enthusiasts, and retro gaming fans who want a quick, engaging tactical draft experience to build and simulate an undefeated 38-0-0 season.

## Product Purpose

A retro football draft simulator where players spin for historical Premier League club-seasons, draft balanced 11-player lineups within tactical formations, and simulate a full 38-game season against authentic historical opponents.

## Brand Personality

- Retro-tactical nostalgia
- Refined Starbucks-inspired palette (#006241 green, warm #f2f0eb parchment, gold accents)
- Tactile, punchy, arcade-satisfying micro-interactions

## Anti-references

- Loud casino/gambling wheel interfaces with abrasive flashing lights
- Cluttered esports dashboards with tiny, unreadable stat walls
- Washed-out low-contrast AI boilerplate themes

## Design Principles

1. **Game HUD Purity**: Live campaign metrics (Record, Gameweek, Points) occupy an uncluttered status zone; external links and secondary chrome must never compete with active game state.
2. **Tactical Legibility**: Formations, pitch slots, card ratings (OVR), and positional compatibilities must be readable at a glance on mobile and desktop.
3. **Tactile Delight**: Drag-and-drop drafting, reel spins, and match day rollouts should feel snappy, physical, and rewarding.
4. **Frictionless Sharing**: Campaign outcomes generate clean, self-contained summary cards ready for instant social sharing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA contrast compliance across all text on dark green pitch and card backgrounds
- Minimum 44x44px touch targets on mobile pitch slots and action buttons
- Clean semantic HTML structure with screen-reader accessible SVGs and navigation landmarks
