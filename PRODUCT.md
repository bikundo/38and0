# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Soccer fans, football manager enthusiasts, and Premier League followers who want to build a dream squad and test if their combination can go completely unbeaten (38-0-0 or 0-loss season) against simulated Premier League opposition.

## Product Purpose
Provide an immersive, fast-paced squad-drafting and match-simulation game. The goal is to draft a squad of 11 Premier League starters (filtered to elite players from top clubs) and simulate a full 38-game season, showing match-by-match results, season statistics, and a final summary of how close they came to going unbeaten.

## Brand Personality
Engineered, professional, editorial, developer-friendly, sleek. Inspired by HashiCorp's dark canvas visual system with a near-black canvas, clean typography, precise 1px translucent borders, and product-specific color highlights (used for game stages/states).

## Anti-references
Bright/neon consumer apps, over-rounded bubbles, generic SaaS cream/sand color palettes, gradient text, excessive emojis, messy list displays, and crowded dashboards.

## Design Principles
1. **Dark Editorial Focus**: Rely on a rich dark grey/black ground with clean typographic hierarchy.
2. **Per-Stage Identity**: Use distinct accents (e.g. Terraform Purple, Waypoint Cyan) to mark different phases of the game (Drafting, Simulation, Results).
3. **Structured Spacing**: Clear visual layout separating the Sidebar controls, the Simulation controls, the Match Feed, and the Pitch.
4. **Contrast & Clutter-free**: Clear text colors (ink, ink-muted) with sufficient contrast and clean borders.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Clear contrast ratios (>= 4.5:1 for body and normal text).
- Responsive grid behaviors down to mobile/tablet breakpoints.
- Clear and predictable focus states/interactive elements.
